01 — About

Designer’s eye, engineer’s discipline.

I started as a graphic designer in Porto and learned to code so my layouts could move. Eleven years later I build the parts of products people remember: onboarding that feels effortless, dashboards that explain themselves, and WebGL moments that make a brand feel alive.

Today I work independently with product teams and studios across four continents. I care about the invisible details — focus states, reduced-motion fallbacks, 60 fps on mid-range phones — because that is where craft turns into trust.

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind?

I build interactive marketing sites, product interfaces, WebGL experiences and design systems. Most projects are with product teams, studios and agencies who want something that feels crafted but stays fast and accessible.

Focused projects such as a landing page with a WebGL hero usually start around €12,000. Larger product work is planned in two-week sprints; I share a fixed estimate after a free 30-minute discovery call.

A marketing site usually takes four to eight weeks from kickoff to launch. Product and design-system work runs in iterative sprints, with something usable in production within the first month.
