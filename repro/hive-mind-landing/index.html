      Questions

      Reasonable questions, honest answers.

      Can’t find yours? Wonder it out loud once you’ve synced — about 40,000 members work in customer support.

      Will I lose my personality?

      No. Quorum is a link, not a blender. Members score 100% themselves on the Individuality Index before and after syncing. You’ll still be you — just better informed.

      So can everyone read my mind?

      Pretty much everyone can access what you’ve chosen to share: skills, languages, know-how — the stuff you’d happily explain at a party. Memories and hot takes are private until you say otherwise, and your inner monologue can’t be shared at all.

      What actually happens on Hive Day?

      From 9 to 5 on your chosen day you work on objectives voted in by quorum — mapping, teaching, research, restoration and the occasional collective crossword. You’ll feel pleasantly purposeful. At 5 p.m. it stops. Mid-sentence, if necessary.

      What if I’m sick, on holiday, or just not feeling very hive-y?

      You get 25 Hive Days off a year, plus sick days. Another member covers for you — they’ll remember exactly how you’d have done it.

      Is the 5% before or after tax?

      Before. It’s collected monthly, scales with what you earn and drops to zero if you earn nothing. Nobody is ever priced out of knowing things.

      Can I leave?

      Any time, with 30 days’ notice. You keep everything you learned about yourself. Borrowed skills fade over about a month, like a language you stop practising.

      Is this a cult?

      Cults don’t have a 9-to-5, a published fee schedule or a cancellation policy. We have all three. Also, cults hate it when you ask. We genuinely don’t mind.

      Resistance is optional
