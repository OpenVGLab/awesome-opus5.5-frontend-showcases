About this atlas

Nothing here is real. All of it is live.

Each world is drawn by its own procedural shader — continents, gas bands, crystal facets and glowing seams are computed from noise every frame. There are no textures, images or sound files: the star, the sky, the soundscapes and even the thumbnails are generated when the page loads.

Explore

Drag to orbit the system and click a world to land. Switch between visible, thermal and elevation scans, and tap numbered markers to visit points of interest.

Compare

Line every world up at its true relative size — with Earth for scale — and re-sort them by gravity, temperature, day length or distance.

Forge

Design your own planet: pick a type and shape, tune seas, storms and glow, then add it to the atlas or save a postcard.
