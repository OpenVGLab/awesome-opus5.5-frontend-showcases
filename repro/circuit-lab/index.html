How to use Circuit Lab

1 · Build

Drag batteries, resistors, bulbs, switches, meters and wires from the left onto the workspace (or click a card to add one).

Drag a part to move it. Drag a wire end to stretch the wire, or drag from any free terminal to draw a new wire.

When two metal ends touch, a gold contact point appears automatically – that is where current can pass.

2 · Set up

Double-click any part to set its properties: battery voltage, resistance, bulb rating, meter range or wire colour.

Click a switch to open or close it. Select a part to rotate or delete it.

3 · Test

Press Start Test on the right. A closed circuit shows the current intensity, moving current dots and every reading.

If the circuit is open, the disconnected spots are marked in red and explained in the list.
