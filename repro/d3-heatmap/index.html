How it works

  1. Bin. One pass over typed arrays drops every point into a cell and accumulates count, Σv, Σv², min and max.
  2. Aggregate. The chosen statistic becomes the cell value; cells with too few points stay empty (hatched).
  3. Colour. A d3 sequential or diverging scale maps values to colours; d3.contours traces the isolines.
  4. Zoom. d3.zoom re-bins the visible window from the raw points on every frame.
